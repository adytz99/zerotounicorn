How to get the app

Zero to Unicorn is on iOS. Get it from the App Store.

Account / save

Sign in with email in the app. Progress syncs in the cloud.

Delete account

Open Settings in the app. Your auth email is removed. Game progress can be restored for 30 days with a one-time code.

Download my data

Open Settings in the app.

Purchases

Purchases are managed by Apple. Restore purchases in the app after reinstalling or changing devices.
